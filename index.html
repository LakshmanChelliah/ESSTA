Our Roots

Tamil Heritage & History

An Ancient Language

Tamil is one of the oldest languages in human history. Those who speak Tamil are called Thamilar or Tamils. For more than 17 centuries, South India and the Northern and Eastern parts of Sri Lanka have been the traditional homelands of Tamils.

A Global Journey

About one thousand years ago, Tamils from South India crossed the high seas on trade and military missions to Myanmar, Malaysia, Indonesia, Cambodia, and beyond. Sri Lankan Tamils mainly remained in their traditional homeland of Sri Lanka.

Colonial Era & Migration

During the British Colonial era, a small number of Sri Lankan Tamils migrated to Malaysia and Singapore, while Tamils from South India were brought to Sri Lanka by the British Colonial administration to work in tea plantations in the hill country.

Independence & Emigration

Following independence in 1948, Sri Lanka became a self-governing country and Sri Lankan Tamils continued to live as a minority community. Over the last four decades, Sri Lankan Tamils began emigrating to Western countries in significant numbers, settling in Canada, the UK, Australia, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Norway, Denmark, and beyond.

Our Home

The Sri Lankan Tamil Community in Edmonton

A small fraction of those who settled in Canada moved further west to Edmonton. We are proud to say that Sri Lankan Tamils in Edmonton have contributed more than their share of knowledge and service to Edmonton, Alberta, and Canada as a whole.

The time has come, and they are reaching their retirement age and are looking forward to enjoying the retirement they have earned.

"Contributing knowledge and service to Edmonton, Alberta, and Canada, now embracing a well-earned retirement together."

Our Mission

About ESSTA

The purpose of the Edmonton Senior Sri Lankan Tamils Association (ESSTA) is to facilitate seniors to lead a healthy and vibrant life through social participation and engagement.

ESSTA brings together Edmonton's senior Sri Lankan Tamil community, honoring our shared heritage while building meaningful connections for the years ahead.

What We Do

Our Programs & Objectives

ESSTA designs and delivers a mix of programs aligned with our purpose, including the following, and more as our community grows.

  • 01

    Regular Gatherings

    Providing facilities to meet on a regular basis and stay connected with one another.

  • 02

    Health & Wellness

    Providing professional information on health, diet, physical exercise, and well-being.

  • 03

    Companionship & Support

    Providing companionship and moral support in times of need.

  • 04

    Outings & Tours

    Arranging outings and tours suitable and enjoyable for seniors.

  • 05

    Cultural Heritage

    Acquainting and promoting ethnic cultures to younger generations.
